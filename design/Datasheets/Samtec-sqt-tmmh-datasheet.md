4.4 Maximum Reflow Passes: The parts can withstand three reflow passes at a maximum component temperature of
260°C.

4.5 Stencil Thickness: The stencil thickness is 0.15mm [.006”].

4.6 Placement: Machine placement of the parts in the Z-axis is strongly recommended.

4.7 Reflow Environment: Samtec recommends the use of a low level oxygen environment (typically achieved through
Nitrogen gas infusion) in the reflow process to improve solderability.

4.8 Hardware: Board-to-board standoffs are recommended to provide a robust mechanical connection. Samtec offers
two different types:

4.8.1 Traditional Standoffs (SO) – Rigid design to statically support board-to-board applications. See
options here: SO - Board Stacking Standoff

4.8.2 Jack Screw Standoffs (JSO) – Serve same function as traditional standoffs but unique, nested
construction facilitates the mating and unmating process. This is especially helpful for multiple
connector applications where the mating and unmating forces increase with the number of
connectors used. See options here: JSO - Jack Screw Standoffs

4.9 Cleaning: Samtec, Inc. has verified that our connectors may be cleaned in accordance with the solvents
 and conditions designated in the EIA-364-11 standard.
