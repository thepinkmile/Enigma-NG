## Reviewed Summary

- **Exact family:** Samtec SQT-1XX-XX-XXX-X-XXX-XXX series
- **Product:** 30-position (15 × 2) shrouded rugged PCB socket, female IDC, right-angle mount, 2.00 mm pitch (0.0787 in)
- **Mating:** Mates with 2BHR-XX-VUA male headers (same pitch, dual-row configuration)
- **Contacts:** Brass, gold-flash plated
- **Insulation:** PBT (polybutylene terephthalate)
- **Electrical:** 2 A per contact, 500 V AC (1 min dielectric), 100-cycle durability per IPC-1212
- **Operating range:** −40°C to +105°C
- **Compliance:** Lead-free, RoHS-compliant
