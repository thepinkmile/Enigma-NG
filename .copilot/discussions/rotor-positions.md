## 🟢 VARIANT 1: 64-CHARACTER BASE-64 ENCODER (0.000° REFERENCE)

* **Step Size:** 5.625° per step
* **Sensor Tracking Setup:** 
  * Front Track Sensors `[F1, F2, F3]` are locked to physical index columns `00, 01, 02`.
