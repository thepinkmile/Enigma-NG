# CPLD Stepping Control Subsystem Design Specification
## Project: Generic Modular Cipher Machine (SIGABA Variant Logic)

This document contains the complete mathematical, logical, and structural specifications for compiling the 4-bit dynamic control bank feedback loop into a CPLD framework. This architecture manages the independent actuation and dual-rail direction configuration for 5 micro-solenoid driven code wheels (Rotor 4 to Rotor 0).

---

## 1. Mathematical Framework and Vector Construction

The subsystem derives its routing parameters from a 4-bit combinational tracking window comprising 2 pre-index scrambled bits and 2 post-index transformed bits.

### 1.1 Input Defintions
Let the raw status inputs be represented as:
~PRE_1~  : Pre-index High Bit
~PRE_0~  : Pre-index Low Bit
~POST_1~ : Post-index High Bit
~POST_0~ : Post-index Low Bit

### 1.2 Interleaved Arrays
Two independent asymmetric 4-bit binary values are generated by cross-shuffling the inputs:
~Vector_A = [ pre0, post0, pre1, post1 ]~
~Vector_B = [ post1, pre0, post0, pre1 ]~

### 1.3 Asymmetric Binary Summation
Vector A and Vector B are interpreted as standard unsigned 4-bit integers and summed. This arithmetic addition expands the range into a 5-bit control word, ~Out[4:0]~:
~Out[4:0] = Vector_A + Vector_B~
Maximum Range: ~15 + 15 = 30~ (~0x1E~, requiring 5 active bits).

---

## 2. Electromechanical Output Abstraction Layers

The CPLD directly maps the raw combinational sum (~Out[4:0]~) into isolated Step and Direction pins across the 5 physical rotor modules. 

### 2.1 Center-Outward Actuation Mapping
To optimize current distribution and maintain the center-outward mechanical footprint, the bits of ~Out[4:0]~ route to the individual step lines (~S4~ to ~S0~) using the following assignment:

* **~Out4~** -> **~S2~** (Center Rotor / Middle Wheel)
* **~Out3~** -> **~S4~** (Left Outer Rotor / Slowest)
* **~Out2~** -> **~S0~** (Right Outer Rotor / Fastest)
* **~Out1~** -> **~S3~** (Left Inner Rotor)
* **~Out0~** -> **~S1~** (Right Inner Rotor)

*Logic Value:* ~1~ = Pulse 3.3V Micro-Solenoid (Step Wheel); ~0~ = Drive Line Low (Halt Wheel).

### 2.2 Directional Inversion Grid
The direction state for the four outer rotors (~D4, D3, D1, D0~) is derived by isolating the lower 4 bits of the sum (~Out[3:0]~) and performing a complete bit-reversal operation:
~Reversed_Value = [ Out0, Out1, Out2, Out3 ]~

The resulting 0 to 15 value maps directly to a 4-axis direction matrix where:
~1~ = Forward Direction
~0~ = Reverse Direction

### 2.3 Center Parity & Inverse Average Logic
The Center Rotor Direction (~D2~) dynamically acts as a mechanical balancer, deriving its movement state from the algebraic sum of the outer four direction vectors. Each outer forward state counts as ~+1~ and each reverse counts as ~-1~:

~Net_Direction_Sum = Direction(R4) + Direction(R3) + Direction(R1) + Direction(R0)~

* **Condition ~Sum > 0~:** Outer wheels are predominantly Forward. Center steps **Reverse (~D2 = 0~)**.
* **Condition ~Sum < 0~:** Outer wheels are predominantly Reverse. Center steps **Forward (~D2 = 1~)**.
* **Condition ~Sum == 0~:** (Perfect 2-Forward / 2-Reverse Tie). System forces the tie-break default: Center steps **Forward (~D2 = 1~)**.

## 4. Hardware Implementation & Boolean Simplification

To compile this design onto a standard high-density CPLD or FPGA structural array without wasting macrocell logic blocks, the outputs can be implemented via minimized Sum-of-Products (SOP) logic equations. 

### 4.1 Step Solenoid Boolean Equations
By extracting the truth values matching target ~High~ outcomes, the minimized logic primitives are defined as:

* **~S2 (Center Rotor)~**
  ~S2 = (P1 & ~P0) | (P0 & ~T1 & T0) | (P1 & P0 & T1 & ~T0) | (P0 & T1 & ~T0 & ~P1)~

* **~S4 (Left Outer)~**
  ~S4 = (P0 & T1 & T0 & ~P1) | (P1 & ~P0 & T1 & ~T0) | (P1 & P0 & ~T1) | (P1 & P0 & T1 & T0)~

* **~S0 (Right Outer)~**
  ~S0 = (~P1 & ~P0 & T1 & ~T0) | (~P1 & P0 & ~T1 & ~T0) | (P1 & ~P0 & T1 & ~T0) | (P1 & P0 & T1 & T0)~

* **~S3 (Left Inner)~**
  ~S3 = (~P1 & ~P0 & T0) | (~P1 & P0 & T1 & ~T0) | (P1 & ~P0 & ~T1 & T0) | (P1 & ~P0 & T1 & ~T0) | (P1 & P0 & T1 & ~T0)~

* **~S1 (Right Inner)~**
  ~S1 = (~P1 & ~P0 & T0) | (~P1 & P0 & ~T1 & ~T0) | (~P1 & P0 & T1 & ~T0) | (P1 & ~P0 & T0) | (P1 & P0 & ~T1) | (P1 & P0 & T1 & ~T0)~

### 4.2 Direction H-Bridge Boolean Equations
The directional output circuits require permanent steady-state signals mapping cleanly to the H-Bridge switching pairs:

* **~D4 (Rotor 4 Dir)~**
  ~D4 = (~T1 & ~T0) | (~P0 & T1 & ~T0) | (P1 & T1 & ~T0) | (P0 & T1 & T0 & ~P1)~

* **~D3 (Rotor 3 Dir)~**
  ~D3 = (~T1 & ~T0) | (~P1 & P0 & ~T1) | (P1 & ~P0 & T1) | (P0 & T1 & T0 & ~P1)~

* **~D1 (Rotor 1 Dir)~**
  ~D1 = (~T1 & ~T0) | (~P1 & ~P0 & T0) | (P1 & ~P0 & ~T1) | (P0 & T1 & T0 & ~P1)~

* **~D0 (Rotor 0 Dir)~**
  ~D0 = (~T1 & ~T0) | (~P1 & ~P0 & T0) | (~P1 & P0 & ~T1) | (P1 & P0 & ~T1 & T0)~

* **~D2 (Center Rotor Dir)~**
